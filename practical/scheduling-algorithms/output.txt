SCHEDULING ALGORITHMS PRACTICAL
================================

1. FCFS SCHEDULING
------------------

Command:
./fcfs

Input:
Number of processes: 3
P1: 5
P2: 3
P3: 8

P2      3       5       8
P3      8       8       16
